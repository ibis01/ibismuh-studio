     1→     1→# TubeForge — Production-Readiness Roadmap (governing, adopted 2026-11-13)
     2→     2→
     3→     3→**Formal decision (adopted at S98, HEAD `d023a01`):** major feature development
     4→     4→is FROZEN. Until every gate below is closed, the only allowed changes are
     5→     5→security fixes, reliability improvements, bug fixes, and maintenance. No new
     6→     6→video engine starts before the exit criteria pass.
     7→     7→
     8→     8→The 13-engine roadmap is complete. TubeForge's next competitive advantage is
     9→     9→operational reliability, not another AI engine. Each stage below lands as a
    10→    10→numbered worklog stage with the established discipline: unit pins, full local
    11→    11→gates, and a CI-green push before the stage is marked done.
    12→    12→
    13→    13→## Why (the evidence)
    14→    14→
    15→    15→The S60 production incident proved that a Postgres-only regression can ship
    16→    16→with zero automated coverage when every CI surface runs SQLite — the S61 lane
    17→    17→closed that specific hole, but the pattern generalizes. The queue's recovery
    18→    18→logic still assumes same-host claimants (insufficient for distributed
    19→    19→workers), storage quota checks are read-check-write rather than atomic
    20→    20→reservations, the rate limiter is an in-memory Map with a spoofable
    21→    21→`X-Forwarded-For` trust boundary, production CSP still allows
    22→    22→`unsafe-inline`/`unsafe-eval`, and the Dockerfile's fidelity to a real
    23→    23→deployment is unevidenced. These are the classes of failure that cost users,
    24→    24→not features that attract them.
