   165→## §6 Anti-duplication findings (the "don't build a second engine" checklist)
   166→
   167→1. **Four camera models coexist**: `anim/camera.ts` (rich, dormant), `graphics/camera.ts` (S130 pan/zoom), whiteboard camera director + drift, `scene3d.ts cameraAt` (linear). The directive's "neither Three.js nor Blender introduces an independent timeline/clock" is already house law — the MG-4/MG-1 work should **unify camera vocabulary incrementally** (extend `scene3d` keys with ease; consider `anim/camera.ts` as the eventual shared vocabulary) rather than adding a fifth.
   168→2. **Motion presets must be data, not a framework**: a preset registry on `EASE_CURVES`/`evalAt`/`ElementTween` (the directive's Opus-derived idea) requires zero new runtime — presets compile into existing tween directives.
   169→3. **Lip-sync already exists beat-sync**: do not rebuild viseme pipelines; the scope claim is pinned in grammar, UI, and audits. Phoneme lip-sync would be a scope change, not a gap fix.
   170→4. **Voice-over sync already exists**: W4 word anchors + captions ASR/silencedetect ladder are landed; Motion Canvas's voice-sync value is already internalized.
   171→5. **The "AI Director determines why/when/how" requirement is partially met** (semantic directives, beat classifier, rhetorical layer, advisory engine pick) — the missing half is *consumption by lanes* (rhetorical purposes → gestures; cues → SFX), which is exactly MG-3/MG-2 wiring, not new architecture.
